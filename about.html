About

About the publication and research archive

An independent open-access academic publication and archive of scholarly works on regenerative endodontics, oral rehabilitation, clinical artificial intelligence and scientific research methods.

Aims and scope

The site makes accessible dental-regeneration research alongside selected related scholarship in clinical technology, prosthodontics and AI-supported scientific methods. Each work has its own landing page, identifier and PDF.

Hosting a work here does not imply an editorial acceptance decision, external peer review, formal journal indexing, or DOI registration by this website. The source identified by the DOI, when available, is cited on each article page.
