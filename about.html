About the journal

Open Source Journal of Dental Regeneration & Biology

An independent open-access publication focused on dental regeneration, regenerative endodontics, pulp biology, tissue engineering and related areas of oral biology.

Aims and scope

The journal provides a public platform for scholarly articles, critical reviews and research-oriented academic contributions in dental regeneration and oral biology.

Information about identifiers, review status and indexing is stated transparently on the editorial information page and, where applicable, on each individual article.
